# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Aurora Survival serves iPhone and iPad owners preparing for outdoor situations where connectivity may be unavailable. The public website also needs to establish product and team credibility for partners, startup programs, and early users.

## Product Purpose

Aurora Survival is a fully offline field assistant combining local model inference, a reviewed survival manual, signed offline maps, and on-device species identification. It is an educational aid, not a replacement for emergency services, certified first-aid training, or professional judgment.

## Positioning

Aurora keeps questions and selected photos on the device while grounding relevant answers in exact passages from its reviewed offline manual.

## Operating Context

People prepare models and map packages while connected, then use Ask, Manual, Maps, and Tools when a network may be weak or absent.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- iPhone and iPad application with four primary areas: Ask, Manual, Maps, and Tools.
- The manual includes 1,050 indexed passages; the species scanner covers 504 North American animals.
- Model and map downloads are the only network-capable product flows and occur only during explicit Preparation mode.
- The App Store release is not public yet. Public calls to action must say “Coming to the App Store” rather than implying availability.
- The website is a static public marketing surface with no account, waitlist, form, or persistent user data.

## Brand Commitments

- Public product name: Aurora Survival.
- Use the existing Aurora icon and its teal, blue, and indigo atmosphere.
- Present the project as independent and founder-led without publishing the founder’s name in the main page copy.
- Voice is plain, credible, restrained, and specific; avoid generic AI language and inflated safety claims.

## Evidence on Hand

- The running Aurora 1.1.0 build is installed on a paired iPhone 13.
- The repository contains the production app icon and current app implementation.
- No testimonials, customer logos, download counts, or public App Store reviews are available and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Work after the signal disappears.
- Keep private field context on the device.
- Show the source when the manual informs an answer.
- Make preparation and runtime limitations explicit.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic landmarks, meaningful image alternatives, and readable mobile layouts.
