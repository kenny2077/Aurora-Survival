# CHAPTER 1 --- FIRECRAFT

## CORE SURVIVAL SKILL:

## BUILD, CONTROL, USE, AND MAINTAIN FIRE

Fire is a survival multiplier.

A controlled fire can provide:

-   Heat and protection from cold
-   Drying for wet clothing and equipment
-   Cooking capability
-   Water heating and purification support
-   Light during darkness
-   A psychological anchor during emergencies
-   A visible rescue signal when conditions allow

A survival fire is not measured by size.

A useful survival fire is:

-   reliable;
-   controlled;
-   fuel efficient;
-   appropriate for the environment.

Always check local fire regulations, wildfire conditions, and
restrictions before using an outdoor fire. Fire restrictions can change
rapidly because of weather and fuel conditions.

# THE FIRE TRIANGLE

Every fire requires three elements:

## 1. HEAT

Heat starts combustion.

Common ignition sources:

-   lighter;
-   waterproof matches;
-   ferrocerium rod;
-   friction ember.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. FUEL

Fuel provides material for combustion.

Prepare fuel in three stages.

## Tinder

The first material to ignite.

Examples:

-   dry grass;
-   bark fibers;
-   fine wood shavings;
-   dry plant fibers;
-   commercial fire starters.

## CORE SURVIVAL SKILL:

## FIND, COLLECT, PURIFY, AND MANAGE WATER

Water is one of the most important survival resources.

The body requires water for:

-   temperature regulation;
-   circulation;
-   physical performance;
-   mental function;
-   normal body processes.

A survival water system has four stages:

1.  FIND water
2.  COLLECT water
3.  TREAT water
4.  STORE water

The objective is not simply finding water.

The objective is obtaining water that is safe to drink.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# THE WATER SURVIVAL PRINCIPLE

Natural water may contain:

-   bacteria;
-   viruses;
-   parasites;
-   sediment;
-   chemical contamination.

Clear water is not automatically safe.

Water treatment methods solve different problems:

## Filtration

Removes particles and some organisms depending on filter design.

## Disinfection

Kills or inactivates many microorganisms.

## Boiling

Kills disease-causing organisms through heat.

No treatment method makes chemically contaminated water safe. Water
containing fuel, toxic chemicals, or radioactive materials requires a
different source. citeturn0search0

# SKILL 2 --- UNDERSTANDING HEAT LOSS

The body loses heat through several processes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CONDUCTION

Heat moves from your body into colder surfaces.

Example:

Sleeping directly on cold ground.

Solution:

Create insulation underneath your body.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# CONVECTION

Moving air removes heat.

Example:

Cold wind passing over exposed skin.

Solution:

Block wind.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# EVAPORATION

Water leaving the body removes heat.

Example:

Wet clothing in cold conditions.

Solution:

Keep clothing dry.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# RADIATION

The body naturally releases heat into the environment.

Solution:

Add insulation and protection.
