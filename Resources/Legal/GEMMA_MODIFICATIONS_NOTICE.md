# Gemma model modifications

The distributed `gemma-3-1b-it-Q4_K_M.gguf` is derived from Gemma 3 1B IT and
was converted to GGUF and quantized to Q4_K_M for local inference. Aurora did
not train the upstream Gemma model. The signed manifest identifies the pinned
upstream repository and revision and records the exact distributed-file hash.

Gemma is subject to the Gemma Terms and the incorporated Gemma Prohibited Use
Policy. Production redistribution remains blocked until professional legal
review approves Aurora's enforceable terms flow and release package.
