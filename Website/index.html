The useful details.

Does Aurora work without a connection?

Yes. Once the selected model and map packages are prepared, Aurora’s guidance, Manual, maps, and species scanner run locally.

How is Aurora’s content verified?

Manual content carries source metadata and exact passage links, map packages are signed before activation, and the current build was validated on a physical iPhone 13.

Which devices are supported?

Aurora is being built for iPhone and iPad.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. Aurora Survival is coming to the App Store. Contact Aurora for product and partnership conversations.
