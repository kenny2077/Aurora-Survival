The useful details.

Does Aurora work without a connection?

Yes. Once the selected model and map packages are prepared, Aurora’s guidance, Manual, maps, and species scanner run locally.

Where do answers come from?

When the Manual informs an answer, Aurora can link back to the exact offline passages used for grounding.

Which devices are supported?

Aurora is being built for iPhone and iPad. Its Lite model targets iPhone 13-class devices and comparable hardware.

Can I download it now?

Not yet. Aurora Survival is coming to the App Store. Contact Aurora for product and partnership conversations.
