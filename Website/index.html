Survival Basics / Stop and Control Panic

  1. Take slow breaths and say out loud what happened.
  2. Move only far enough to escape an immediate threat such as traffic, fire, falling rock, or rising water.
  3. Mark where you stopped and note the time, weather, and last place you were certain of your location.

Do not run downhill or follow water just because it appears to lead out.

Primary sources: U.S. National Park Service; U.S. Department of the Army, ATP 3-50.21

Find and Treat Water / Boil Water Correctly

  1. Bring clear water to a rolling boil for 1 minute.
  2. Above 6,500 feet (about 1,980 metres), boil for 3 minutes.
  3. Let it cool and store it in a clean, covered container without touching the inside of the cap.

Boiling does not remove fuel, toxic chemicals, salt, or radioactive contamination.

Primary sources: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention

Signal for Rescue / Signal With a Whistle

  1. Give three strong blasts, pause, listen, and repeat at regular intervals.
  2. Move only to a nearby safe opening or ridge if it clearly improves the signal.
  3. Keep the whistle attached to clothing so it remains available after a fall or lost pack.

Do not blow continuously until exhausted or unable to hear a reply.

Primary sources: U.S. National Park Service; U.S. Department of the Army, ATP 3-50.21

Weather and Wildlife / Act During Lightning

  1. Leave ridges, peaks, open water, open fields, and isolated tall objects immediately.
  2. If no safe building or vehicle exists, spread a group apart and move toward lower terrain away from water and tall isolated trees.
  3. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder before leaving safe shelter.

A tent, cave entrance, picnic shelter, cliff overhang, or isolated tree is not reliable lightning protection.

Primary sources: NOAA National Weather Service

How is Aurora’s content verified?

Manual content carries source metadata and exact passage links, map packages are signed before activation, and the current build was validated on a physical iPhone 13.
